    Draft log

      League settings

      Draft order

      From your league charter: 15 rounds fill 10 starters (PG, SG, SF, PF, C, G, F, 3 UTIL) and 5 bench spots. IR spots aren't drafted.

      Scoring format

      How your league decides who wins. Check your league site if you're not sure. Most leagues are head-to-head categories.

      League site

      Where your league drafts. Managers lean on that site's rankings, so it decides who's likely still there at your picks. Yahoo and ESPN disagree a lot on some players (Kawhi Leonard goes around pick 17 on Yahoo and 28 on ESPN; ESPN's category rankings have him 18th).

      Your leaguemates

      How the other managers pick. It decides who's likely still there at your picks ("% still there" and the simulations). Casual managers take players straight off ESPN's list; experienced category-league drafters follow the expert consensus (FantasyPros, Yahoo's analysts), so players ESPN underrates go much sooner.

      Practice opponents

      How the other teams draft when you press Sim to my pick. Smart opponents use the same logic the app uses for you, each with his own plan and his own take on future seasons (a bit slower). Typical opponents draft off ESPN's rankings, like most casual managers.

      Punt strategy

      Pick categories you plan to give up on. The advice will favour players who are strong elsewhere and weak only in those categories. Leave all off for a balanced team.

      Future seasons

      This is a keeper league: after the season you keep up to 6 players, each for up to 3 seasons. The app values young players who should improve, and discounts older ones, based on how 9-cat value changes with age across five NBA seasons. How much should next year count against this one?

      Keepers

      2026-27 is the league's first season, so nobody has keepers yet and there's nothing to enter this year. Everyone you draft now can be kept next season. Before next year's draft, add every team's keepers here.

      Each keeper costs one round earlier than where he was drafted, then one round earlier again for each extra year he's kept (never earlier than round 1, and 3 years at most). Waiver and free-agent pickups cost the last-round pick. If two keepers cost the same round, the second one uses the team's next open pick. Keeper picks are skipped automatically during the draft.

      Reset

      Clears every pick so you can start over. Keepers and settings stay.
